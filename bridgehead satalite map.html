SETTINGS
Order arrowsO
Arrows show where your units are going. Solid while paused, faint while the clock runs.
Fog of warV
Enemies are hidden until your units can see them. Off shows every unit, for both sides.
Map viewT
Satellite imagery, or the strategic map (the original printed-map look). Only the drawing changes; the battle is the same.
Line of control styleL
One shared front line, or a line for each side with no-man's land between them. L shows or hides it.
