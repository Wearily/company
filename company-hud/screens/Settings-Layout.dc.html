Interface layout: Floating or Docked

Players choose in Settings. Floating is the default. Both layouts use the same components, rounding and shortcuts, and both work in Light or Dark. Only where the bars sit changes.

A · Floating, LightDefault
C · Floating, Dark
D · Docked, Light
B · Docked, Dark

Stored per player and remembered between battles. Changing it takes effect at once, even mid-battle; the clock keeps running. On phones, Floating keeps the inset rounded sheets, and Docked pins the top bar and order bar edge to edge.
